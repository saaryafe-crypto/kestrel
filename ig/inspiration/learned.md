- Scale mismatch: tiny actor, huge outcome ("ONE PERSON. MILLIONS IN REVENUE. NO EMPLOYEES") — the Medvi register

- Human-impact AI news: jobs, money, family, viral moments — not corporate press releases
- Drama angles: rogue agents, bans, lawsuits, "planned all along" conspiracies

## Register
- Young, direct, zero corporate phrasing; tabloid energy but every fact true
