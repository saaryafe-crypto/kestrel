Anthropic is going public at $2 trillion, 'despite AI safety concerns' right in the announcement. 🤯

Anthropic was the one AI company you were supposed to trust. Its founders left OpenAI because they thought OpenAI was being too reckless with safety. That was the entire reason the company existed. Not a marketing line. The reason.

Now they are going public in November. The valuation is $2 trillion. The offering raises up to $100 billion, which would be the largest amount any company has ever raised in a single stock debut. And somewhere in the Wall Street Journal's story about all of this, a reporter wrote the phrase 'despite AI safety concerns.' About the company whose whole identity is AI safety. Nobody killed the line.

Here is the part that should make your head spin: Anthropic is already on track to bring in more than $110 billion in revenue this year. A company that barely existed three years ago now makes more money annually than almost every business on Earth. They do not need the Wall Street money to survive. They want it to grow.

If you were counting on Anthropic to be the voice in the room saying 'let's slow down and be careful,' that voice just got bought. Once you take $100 billion from investors who paid $2 trillion for a piece of you, slowing down is off the table. Anthropic just became structurally unable to do the one thing it was built to do.

Every major AI lab started with a story about being the careful one. One by one, the money rewrites the story. Anthropic was the last one still telling it.
