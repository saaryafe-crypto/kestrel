A Tulsa sheriff's deputy looked up a woman's license plate in the Flock Safety camera network without a warrant. A federal judge ruled that's unconstitutional, police cannot track your car without a warrant. Flock Safety cameras are in thousands of cities across America, which means your car's movements may have been tracked illegally every time a cop ran your plates without a warrant. Police bought these cameras to catch criminals, and a judge just ruled the cops were the ones breaking constitutional law the whole time.

If this holds, police nationwide will need warrants before any plate search, or risk cases getting thrown out.
