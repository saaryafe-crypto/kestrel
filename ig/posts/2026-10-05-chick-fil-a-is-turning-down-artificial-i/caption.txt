Chick-fil-A is keeping humans in the drive-thru, on purpose. 🍗

Chick-fil-A turned down AI drive-thru technology and is keeping real human workers to take your order, while every other major fast food chain races to automate. The brand already famous for saying "my pleasure" looked at the same AI tools and said no. If your job is being replaced by a drive-thru bot somewhere else right now, Chick-fil-A just handed you the loudest counterexample in the industry. The one chain that built its whole identity on human warmth is the only one refusing to swap it for a machine.
