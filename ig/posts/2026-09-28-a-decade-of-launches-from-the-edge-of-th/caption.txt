A sheep farm in New Zealand has launched nearly 100 rockets to orbit in 10 years, including one to the Moon. 🚀

Ten years ago, Rocket Lab turned a sheep farm paddock in New Zealand into the world's first private orbital launch site. Nearly 100 rockets have lifted off from that same paddock, carrying commercial satellites, national security payloads, and one spacecraft all the way to the Moon. New Zealand is now one of the busiest launch countries on Earth, from a paddock where sheep used to graze.
