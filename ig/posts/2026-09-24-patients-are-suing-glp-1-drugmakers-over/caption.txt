Ozempic patients are suing the drugmakers over sudden vision loss 👁️

A year ago, GLP-1 drugs like Ozempic could do no wrong. Millions of people prescribed them. Losing weight, controlling diabetes, feeling better. The side effects everyone talked about were nausea and some stomach issues. Nobody had sudden vision loss anywhere near the top of the worry list.

Now there are lawsuits. Patients who took these drugs are in court claiming they caused sudden vision loss. Not a minor side effect. We're talking about people losing their sight. The Wall Street Journal has stories on the lawsuits, so this is past social media noise.

Here's the part that makes this actually wild: the drugs weren't failing these people. In many of these cases, the drugs were working. The weight was coming off. And at the same time, the vision was going.

Anyone taking Ozempic or one of these drugs right now has a new conversation to have with their doctor. The companies making these drugs are about to spend years in court defending a product that made them billions. You took it to get healthier. You lost your sight. That's the story in court.

Ozempic and drugs like it are among the most prescribed on the planet. When a side effect this serious shows up in court for a drug this popular, nobody comes out the same. Doctors, patients, and companies all have new questions to answer.
