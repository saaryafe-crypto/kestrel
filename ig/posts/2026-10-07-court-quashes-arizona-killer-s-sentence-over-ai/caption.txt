Court quashes Arizona killer's sentence over AI video of dead victim.

An Arizona road-rage killer will be resentenced after an AI video was improperly used at his sentencing.
The victim's sister wrote the script for the video, in which the victim appeared to forgive the killer.
The sentencing judge said he felt "genuine forgiveness" from the AI-generated depiction of the dead victim.
