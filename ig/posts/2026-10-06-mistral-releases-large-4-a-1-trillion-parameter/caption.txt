Mistral releases Large 4, a 1-trillion-parameter open model.

The model has 49 billion active parameters and is natively multimodal.
Mistral says it is the best open-weights model from the US or Europe on aggregated benchmarks.
It surpasses closed frontier models on visual grounding, according to Mistral.
The model is available via API today; open weights are due at the end of October.
