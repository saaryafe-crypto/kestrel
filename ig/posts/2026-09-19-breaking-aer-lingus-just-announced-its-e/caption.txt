Aer Lingus just put free Starlink WiFi on every transatlantic flight, and the whole fleet is already done. ✈️

Up until now, flying across the Atlantic meant either paying for slow WiFi or having none at all. Aer Lingus just changed that overnight.

Their entire A321 fleet is now equipped with Starlink. Not some planes. Not a test group. The whole fleet, already done, and the WiFi is free for every passenger on board.

You can be 35,000 feet over the middle of the Atlantic Ocean, open Netflix, and it just plays. No voucher from the flight attendant, no paywall, no buffering wheel. That is a sentence nobody could write about any major airline six months ago.

The rest of Aer Lingus's long-haul fleet is expected to be connected by the end of 2026. And the thing that finally made airline WiFi good enough to give away for free is Starlink, the satellite internet built by a rocket company. Every airline still charging you $30 for something slower just got embarrassed.

Airlines have promised real in-flight WiFi for twenty years. Starlink is the first technology that actually delivers it, and Aer Lingus is the first major Atlantic carrier to make it free. Every other airline now has a problem.
