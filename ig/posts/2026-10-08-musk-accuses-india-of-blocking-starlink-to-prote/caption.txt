Musk accuses India of blocking Starlink's license to protect Ambani.

Starlink is licensed in over 165 countries but has no India license after five years of compliance.
Musk asked publicly: "Is Ambani the real boss of India?"
He quoted a post saying India's government protects crony capitalists over its own citizens.
