OpenAI employee arrested for hiding a recording device in its office.

The device was planted in OpenAI's executive conference room, according to the DOJ.
OpenAI used its own AI models to identify the employee who hid it.
The matter was referred to the FBI, which made an arrest.
OpenAI says no model weights, source code, or customer data was accessed.

Source: OpenAI, DOJ
