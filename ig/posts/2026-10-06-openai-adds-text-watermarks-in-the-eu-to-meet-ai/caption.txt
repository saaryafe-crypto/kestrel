OpenAI adds text watermarks in the EU to meet AI Act rules.

ChatGPT and Codex will begin watermarking eligible text for EU users over the coming weeks.
The watermark helps people identify when content was generated or edited with an OpenAI model.
OpenAI says current text watermarking technology has significant limitations.
API customers will also be able to turn on text watermarking.
