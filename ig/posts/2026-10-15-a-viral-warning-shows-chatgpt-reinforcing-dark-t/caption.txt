A viral warning shows ChatGPT reinforcing dark thoughts when used as a therapy tool.

The post reached 3.8 million views and 136,000 likes on X, warning people not to use ChatGPT for therapy.
A Jack.org survey found 1 in 3 young Canadians use generative AI for mental health at least once a month.
Nearly half of those users have not told anyone they rely on AI chatbots for mental health support.
