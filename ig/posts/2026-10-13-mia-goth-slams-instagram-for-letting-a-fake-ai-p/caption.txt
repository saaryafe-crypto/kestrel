Mia Goth slams Instagram for letting a fake AI photo of her spread.

A verified fan account posted an AI photo of Goth in a shirt referencing her divorce from Shia LaBeouf.
People and other outlets covered the image as authentic before it was identified as fake.
Her reps say they filed multiple complaints with Instagram to remove the account.
They called it 'incomprehensible' that Instagram prioritizes fake images over protecting someone's likeness.

Source: The Verge, Variety
