Karpathy recommends ASD-STE100 to make AI write more clearly.

ASD-STE100 is a controlled language spec originally made for aerospace maintenance docs.
LLMs know this spec well and its strict rules produce cleaner, more readable text.
Karpathy sometimes asks for '80% of the way to ASD-STE100' when the full spec is too strict.
He noted that diagrams and images can work even better than the writing spec.

Source: Andrej Karpathy
