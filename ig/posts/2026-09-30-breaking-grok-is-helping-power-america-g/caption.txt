Elon Musk's Grok now answers your questions on America.gov, the U.S. government's official AI website.

The U.S. launched America.gov, where you type a question about your taxes, benefits, or immigration status and AI answers you from official government sources. U.S. Chief Design Officer Joe Gebbia confirmed both Grok and Google's Gemini power it. The announcement called it "a major milestone for Grok," framing a public service as a private company's win.
