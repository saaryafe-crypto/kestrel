SpaceX just nailed Starship's flip maneuver and hit the exact splashdown target.

Starship flipped precisely and hit its exact target in the Pacific Ocean on flight 14. Within hours the engineers were already planning flight 15: skip the water, catch the rocket mid-air with robotic arms on the launch tower instead. You could watch a 40-story rocket get snatched from the sky on the very next launch. The ocean landing was the whole goal for years. SpaceX just called it the warm-up.

Every successful Starship test moves reusable rockets closer to being the industry standard, not SpaceX's party trick. A tower catch on flight 15 would put routine rocket recovery on every launch company's roadmap, not just SpaceX's.
