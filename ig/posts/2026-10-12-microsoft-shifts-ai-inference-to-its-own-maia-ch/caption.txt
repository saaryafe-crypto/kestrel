Microsoft shifts AI inference to its own Maia chips starting in 2027.

CEO Satya Nadella announced Microsoft will scale the Maia family to multi-gigawatt deployments next year.
The new Maia 300 chip delivers 40% more tokens per watt than leading GPU-based systems.
Maia 200 already runs Copilot and Teams in the US, Europe, and Australia at up to 1.7x more tokens per watt.
