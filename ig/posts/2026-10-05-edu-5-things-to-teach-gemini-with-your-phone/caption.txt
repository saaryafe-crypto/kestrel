Teaching Gemini with your phone camera is the most underused move in AI right now.

1) Skip the $150 service call. Point at anything broken.
"Walk me through fixing what my camera is showing you."

2) Your form is probably wrong. Film yourself mid-workout.
"Watch me and tell me every mistake in my form."

3) Open the fridge, hold the camera inside for 5 seconds.
"What can I cook tonight with only what you see in my fridge?"

4) Read any language. Sign, menu, anything.
"Translate everything visible in this image into plain English."

5) Show it your sick plant. This one feels like cheating.
"What's wrong with this plant and what should I do?"
