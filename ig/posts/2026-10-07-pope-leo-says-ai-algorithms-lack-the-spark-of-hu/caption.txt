Pope Leo says AI algorithms lack the spark of humanity.

Pope Leo posted on social media that human art and AI output have an ontological difference, before any aesthetic one.
He wrote: "Algorithms lack the spark of humanity."
The post drew more than 25 million views on his official account.
The Church said it wants to renew an alliance with artists and cultural institutions to safeguard humanity.
