OpenAI and Anthropic war-game their response to a 2027 AI catastrophe.

Scenarios include massive cyberattacks enabled by AI products and a political revolt against the companies.
OpenAI said it runs preparedness exercises and does not treat these scenarios as inevitable.
A WSJ poll from late September found 63 percent of US adults support an immediate pause on AI development.
