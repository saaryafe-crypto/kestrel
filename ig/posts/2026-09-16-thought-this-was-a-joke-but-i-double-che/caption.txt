The US government's official law database runs its AI-powered search on Chinese technology, and nobody publicly disclosed it.

The Federal Register is where every federal law and regulation lives. When a lawyer searches for rules that affect your business, or a lobbyist checks what the government just changed, they use this site. The assumption was always that something this official runs on American-built tools.

Someone on X posted that the Federal Register's search mode lists Qwen, a Chinese AI model, as one of its options. The person who found it assumed it was a fake screenshot, opened the actual site to prove it wrong, and found it right there in plain text. They said they double-checked before posting because they were sure it was a joke. If your lawyer hit AI search and picked the Qwen option, that query ran through Chinese servers, and there was nothing on the page that told them so.

This is the same government that spent years trying to ban TikTok on national security grounds. The Federal Register isn't just using a Chinese AI tool. It is running a Chinese AI model directly in the site, meaning someone made an active technical choice to build this in. The government that argued Chinese apps were a national security risk built Chinese AI into the official site where every American law lives.
