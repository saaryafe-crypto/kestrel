Tesla just pushed an update that lets you drive off a Supercharger while the cable is still plugged in. ⚡

Tesla pushed a software update called 'Emergency Drive Away' that lets you drive off a Supercharger while the cable is still in the car. The feature is live now across U.S. Superchargers in update 2026.38.3, and pre-2021 Model S and Model X are not supported. Tesla built the Supercharger hardware to handle it, so the cable just releases on its own as you go. Your Tesla now has a built-in escape from any public charging stop, which quietly reframes every late-night charge as a place you might need to flee.

Tesla now has a purpose-built panic exit engineered into the charging cable itself. When a software update can change what a piece of hardware does overnight, the old rules about product safety change fast.
