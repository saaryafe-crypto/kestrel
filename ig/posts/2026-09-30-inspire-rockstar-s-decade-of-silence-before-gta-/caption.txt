Rockstar's GTA VI map is twice the size of GTA V and three times the playable area of Red Dead 2. Ten years of silence paid off. 🎮

GTA V launched in 2013, earned $1 billion in its first three days, and became one of the best-selling games in history. Then Rockstar went quiet and spent the next decade building the sequel. Now press preview details are out: the new map is twice the size of GTA V, three times the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2, and the main city alone is double the size of Los Santos. They refused to rush. Send this to someone who keeps getting told to just ship it.

Every company is shipping fast and patching later. Rockstar went the other way: ten years, no shortcuts, then something that makes every other open world look small. Patience is still the cheat code.
