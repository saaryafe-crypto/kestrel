Nearly 2 million Americans have been job hunting for over 6 months with nothing to show. 🔴

1.93 million Americans have been searching for work for 27 weeks straight and found nothing. That's the 3rd-worst share of long-term unemployment the US has seen since the 2008 crash. The headline unemployment rate still looks fine, which is exactly why nobody is talking about this. If you've been job hunting for months with nothing to show, you're part of a crisis that's the worst it's been in over a decade. The job market has two versions, and almost 2 million people are stuck in the one nobody reports on.

Long-term unemployment is the story the economy doesn't want to tell. Savings hit zero, benefits run out, and people quietly fall out of the data while the headline numbers stay manageable.
