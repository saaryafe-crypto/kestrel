Elon Musk explained The Boring Company's name is a pun about drilling tunnels, and he had to do it on camera.

The company never seemed worth explaining, until now. Musk sat in front of a camera and decided it needed explaining. He said tunnels are "underappreciated." He pointed to Las Vegas, where the company has working tunnels, and said driving through them feels "like teleporting from one point to another."

The catch is that he had to explain his own company name out loud. The Boring Company. Boring as in dull. Boring as in drilling holes. He built a tunnel startup, named it after a dad joke about drilling, then walked an audience through why that's funny. Six minutes, completely serious.

Tunneling is not a new technology. The pun is basically an admission that digging holes under cities for cars needs a joke to sound interesting.

Every city with bad traffic is now a potential Boring Company pitch. The Vegas Loop is the brochure. The real question is whether the next billion your city spends goes to a private car tunnel or an actual subway.
