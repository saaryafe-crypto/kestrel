Tesla's Megamanifold heats a Semi driver's cabin using the motor's own waste heat, so the heater runs for free.

Electric trucks in cold weather have always had a brutal problem: run the heater, kill your range. That's been the main thing making drivers nervous about electric semis on long winter routes.

Tesla's Semi now captures the heat the motor throws off while running and pipes it straight into the cab. If you're hauling freight through a Minnesota winter, your heater goes full blast and the battery barely moves. That heat was always there. It used to get dumped. Now it keeps the driver warm. Tesla calls the system Megamanifold.

Here's the wild part: waste heat from the motor has always been the enemy. Engineers spend years trying to get rid of it. Tesla just made it the solution. The same single system handles cabin air and motor cooling at the same time. Every electric truck maker competing in cold climates now has a real new bar to clear. The waste heat engineers spent decades trying to dump just became the most useful thing on the truck.

Heat recovery has existed in gas trucks for decades. Building it cleanly into a fully electric system, where everything runs off the same battery, is a different engineering challenge. Tesla just cleared that bar for the Semi, and the rest of the industry is watching.
