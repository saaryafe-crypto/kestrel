A LEGO-head ski mask video on X racked up 5 million views. The entire thing was AI-generated.

Linus Ekenstam posted what looked like a personal gear recommendation for a LEGO-head ski mask to a friend in Sweden and got 5.1 million views and 115K likes. X Community Notes then flagged it: every single image and video in the post was AI-generated, not real footage of anyone skiing. The mask is real and you can actually buy one. But nobody has ever actually been filmed skiing in it.

AI-generated footage now makes fake product videos look completely real, and if you watched that clip, you had no idea the skier, the slope, and the whole scene were made up by AI. 5.1 million people didn't. The mask is real, the skier, the slope, and the whole scene never were.

Sources: LinusEkenstam on X, legohead.co
