Starship reached orbit for the first time, and the closest photo was taken by a camera left alone at the pad.

On September 28, 2026, Starship reached orbit for the first time with all 33 Raptor engines firing clean. Photographer Andrew McCarthy placed his camera right next to the launch pad, set a sound trigger, and walked away because standing there would have killed him. The roar of 33 engines pressed the shutter. His tweet got 587,700 views in hours. Starship is now an orbital rocket.

Right now it costs around $2,000 to send one kilogram to orbit. If SpaceX gets that below $100, the satellites running your GPS, your weather app, and your phone signal all get cheaper to build and replace.
