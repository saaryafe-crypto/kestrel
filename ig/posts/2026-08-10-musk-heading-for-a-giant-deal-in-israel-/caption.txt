SpaceX is buying Israeli AI startup Decart for $7 billion because Musk played their game and typed 'wow' on X. 🧵

Musk tested OASIS, Decart's real-time AI video engine, in 2024, posted one word on X, and reportedly kept in contact with the team ever since. SpaceX is now in advanced talks to acquire the 100-person startup for up to $7 billion. Nvidia, which invested in Decart's $300 million round in May 2026 at a $4 billion valuation, was the frontrunner buyer before SpaceX reportedly outbid them. Decart was founded in 2023 by two Unit 8200 veterans and had raised over $450 million total. If the deal closes, SpaceX would have its first R&D presence in Tel Aviv.
