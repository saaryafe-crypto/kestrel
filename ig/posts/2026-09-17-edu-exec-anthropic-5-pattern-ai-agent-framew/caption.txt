1) Your 4-hour onboarding runs itself, you stack five tasks into one sequence. That's prompt chaining.
"Here is [name]'s role and start date: [text]. Build the onboarding: training plan, week-one schedule, intro email, 30-day milestones. Each step in order."

2) Routing: reads a message, classifies it, writes the right reply. At $12 per ticket, this erases a salary.
"Customer message: [text]. Classify as Complaint, Billing, Product Question, or Refund. Write the right reply."

3) Competitor research used to eat a Friday afternoon. Run all five at once: 12 minutes. That's parallelization.
"5 competitors: [list]. For each: pricing, top 3 weaknesses, one thing they do better than us. Run all five at once."

4) Orchestrator-subagents replaces a team. One agent reads your CRM, dispatches researchers, routes to a writer, schedules sends. Nobody runs it.
"You are an orchestrator. 10 leads: [text]. For each: research the company, write a 3-sentence outreach email, suggest a send time by timezone."

5) Evaluator-optimizer: the $300/hour revision loop runs inside the task. One writes, one grades and rewrites.
"Pitch deck section: [text]. Grade on clarity, specificity, investor appeal. Rewrite anything below
8. Show what changed."
