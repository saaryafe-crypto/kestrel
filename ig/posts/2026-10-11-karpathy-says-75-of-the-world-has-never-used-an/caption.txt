Karpathy says 75% of the world has never used an AI chatbot.

AI researcher Andrej Karpathy estimates about 6 billion people have barely encountered large language models.
Another 1 to 2 billion use free tools like ChatGPT occasionally, mostly as a search engine or writing aid.
Karpathy says the gap in understanding of what AI can do is widening, not closing.

Source: Andrej Karpathy
