Tesla just had its biggest day ever for driverless car sign-ups: 56 Cybercabs registered in one day, pushing the all-time total to 125. 🚗

Yesterday morning, the Cybercab was still basically a car on a stage. No steering wheel, no passengers, just hype and a promise about replacing Uber.

Then someone spent Thursday night refreshing Texas DMV records in real time. One by one, Cybercab registrations ticked up. By the end of the day Tesla had filed 56 of them total, the most ever registered in a single day. The all-time count crossed 100 for the first time, landing at 125.

Here's the part that stops you: 125 cars. That's smaller than the Hertz counter at Dallas airport. Tesla celebrated it like a massive milestone, and sure, it is one. But this is the fleet meant to replace every Uber driver in America, and right now all 125 would fit in one Walmart parking lot.

The numbers are accelerating fast though. If the daily filings keep climbing, you could see one of these no-steering-wheel cabs pull up for a real passenger in Texas soon. No demo, no invite-only test. Just a car with nobody in the front seat.

This is what replacing Uber starts as: 125 cars and one guy watching a DMV database on a Thursday night.

Robotaxis have been "coming soon" for a decade. Tesla is actually filing them with the DMV one by one, and yesterday was the biggest day ever. The pace is clearly accelerating.
