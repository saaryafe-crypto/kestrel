A judge just ruled AI companies can legally destroy books — even ones with almost no copies left on Earth 📚

Anthropic got caught pirating e-books and paid authors $1.5 billion. So the industry found a loophole: buy real books, slice them open with hydraulic cutters, scan every page, and throw the originals away. A judge called it fair use — once you buy a book, it's yours to destroy. A database called ISBNdb now ships AI companies up to one million books per order and keeps every buyer's name secret. Books printed before 2022 are the prize: the last writing on Earth guaranteed to be human.

The companies teaching AI to write are destroying the books humans wrote first — and pre-AI text is vanishing fast.
