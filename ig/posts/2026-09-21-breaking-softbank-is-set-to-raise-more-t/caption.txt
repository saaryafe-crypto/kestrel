SoftBank is raising $11 billion in junk bonds, one of the biggest risky debt deals ever, to keep OpenAI funded. 💸

Everyone assumed the AI boom was backed by tech companies sitting on cash. It's not. Someone at SoftBank sat down, looked at $15 billion in high-risk debt already on the books, and decided the answer was to borrow $11 billion more at the exact same rates. Those rates are called junk-bond rates. Banks use them for companies they aren't confident will pay the money back.

And here's the thing: SoftBank has already sold around $15 billion in these bonds in 2026 alone. That makes it the single biggest junk-rated borrower in all of bond markets right now. Not second. The biggest. And it still needs $11 billion more on top of that.

When this deal closes, SoftBank will have $26 billion in junk bonds on the line for OpenAI this year alone. All riding on one bet: that OpenAI makes enough money fast enough to pay it back. If that doesn't happen, this isn't just a tech problem. That kind of debt spreads. If your savings hold any of that paper, you're in the loss too.

The technology being sold as the most important thing to ever happen to civilization can only stay alive by borrowing at the rates banks charge companies they aren't sure will make it. That's not opinion. That's what the junk-bond rating literally means.

The biggest bets on AI right now aren't being funded by profits. They're being funded by high-risk debt. If the revenue doesn't come soon, this stops being a tech story and becomes a bond crisis.
