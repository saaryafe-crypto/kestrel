Tesla is sold out for 2026 in the U.S. New orders won't arrive until 2027.

Someone sat down last night to finally order that Tesla they'd been putting off. Picked the color, picked the trim. Tesla's website told them: not this year.

The Model 3 RWD is completely gone for 2026 new orders in the U.S. So is the Model Y Launch Series. The Model Y RWD and Model Y AWD are almost gone too. That's nearly every new Tesla you can buy in America, wiped out before the year is even over. Anyone ordering today gets pushed to 2027 automatically.

For years, Tesla was the company that couldn't build cars fast enough. People mocked them constantly for missing targets and pushing delivery dates. Now they've run out of new cars to sell for the entire year. Your options today: wait until 2027, or pay extra on the used market.

Tesla's biggest problem used to be supply. Now it's the opposite.
